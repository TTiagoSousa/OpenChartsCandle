# v1.1.0 · 53 more indicators

### Features

- **indicators:** 53 TradingView-style indicators, written from their public formulas:
  - **averages:** WMA, VWMA, HMA, DEMA, TEMA, SMMA, McGinley Dynamic, ALMA and LSMA;
  - **on the candles:** Bollinger Bands, Keltner Channels, Donchian Channels, Envelopes, Ichimoku Cloud, Williams Alligator, VWAP (with bands), Supertrend and Parabolic SAR;
  - **oscillators:** MACD, Stochastic, Stochastic RSI, ATR, DMI, ADX, CCI, Williams %R, Momentum, ROC, Awesome Oscillator, Aroon, Ultimate Oscillator, TRIX, True Strength Index, Chande Momentum, DPO, Coppock Curve, Fisher Transform, Relative Vigor Index, Choppiness Index, Vortex, Balance of Power, Bollinger %B, Bollinger BandWidth and Historical Volatility;
  - **volume:** Volume, OBV, MFI, Chaikin Money Flow, Accumulation/Distribution, Chaikin Oscillator, Elder Force Index, Ease of Movement and Price Volume Trend.
- **indicators:** each one has TradingView's inputs and a Style tab where every line, level and background has its own show box, colour and width. It can use its own timeframe and updates bar by bar in real time.
- **indicators:** the moving average and the new indicators also accept the sources hl2, hlc3, ohlc4 and hlcc4.
